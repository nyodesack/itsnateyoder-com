The workflow

One completed job. Three thoughtful asks. Zero reliance on memory.

01

Mark the job complete

Your CRM uses the completion event to start the workflow at the emotional high point.

02

Send a personal SMS

Thank the customer by name, reference the team member, and acknowledge the problem solved.

03

Check for the review

If the customer reviewed, stop. If not, send the follow-up email two days later.

04

Send one final reminder

Follow up once more by SMS while the experience is still fresh.

05

Stop or hand off to a human

End the automation or notify the team member who served the customer for a personal touch.
