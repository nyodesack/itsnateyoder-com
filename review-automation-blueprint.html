If there is still no review

Automation should hand off—not become harassment.

After the final SMS, choose a controlled next step instead of sending endless reminders.

Recommended

Trigger a human call

Notify the team member who served the customer. If the experience was positive, ask personally and respectfully.

Alternative

Use a limited reminder track

Move them into a weekly reminder workflow. Stop immediately when they review—or automatically after 30 days.

Implementation rules

The system works because it feels human—not because it sends more messages.

01

Personalize only what matters

First name, team member, and problem solved are enough to make the sequence feel specific.

02

Detect reviews before every send

The fastest way to annoy a happy customer is to keep asking after they already reviewed you.

03

Ask at the emotional high point

Start when the work is marked complete—while the outcome and gratitude are still fresh.

04

Put a hard stop on follow-up

Two automated reminders are enough. After that, use a human touch or end the sequence.
